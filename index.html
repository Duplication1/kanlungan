A shelter for
people who tinker.

Kanlungan started with a simple idea: everyone needs a place to land after the road. Ours is a converted garage where the beans are roasted in small batches and the tools on the wall are real.

Bring your laptop, your sketchbook, your bike club, or your Tita. There's always a stool open and a cup waiting.

  • Small-batch Philippine beans from Benguet, Sagada and Bukidnon
  • Free Wi-Fi and plenty of outlets
  • Bikes and cars welcome in the front lot
